Questions owners ask

Do I need to buy new or smart machines?

No. Spinslot works with the washing machines you already have. Residents enter the duration shown on the machine.

Who can see my machines?

Only verified residents of your PG. Each QR code is linked to one PG and one machine.

What if a machine breaks down?

The owner marks it as Maintenance from the dashboard, and residents see it as unavailable straight away.

Can residents cancel a booking?

Yes. The slot is released and everyone behind it moves up in the queue.

Is the app ready to use?

It is currently under development. Request a demo to see the flow and join the early-access list.
